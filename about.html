About Me

A little background on who I am and what I do.

My Story

I'm Blessed, a second-year BSc Honours student in Data Science and Systems at the University of Zimbabwe. I enjoy turning data into decisions and building tools that solve real problems. I work with Python, Flask, MySQL and Power BI, and I've built full web applications, including a Real Estate Management System. My learning goes beyond the classroom. I hold certifications from ICDL, Future Interns, Kaggle and Harvard's CS50 AI with Python, and I'm currently writing a book on Power BI. Outside of tech, I've worked as Assistant Live Score Manager at the FBC Zimbabwe Open, which taught me to stay accurate and calm under pressure. I'm looking for opportunities to grow, contribute and build things that matter.

What I Care About

I care about using data and technology to make better decisions and solve real problems. Whether I'm building a web application, designing a dashboard or studying machine learning, I want the work to be useful and well built, not just impressive on paper. I also care about sharing what I learn. I'm writing a book on Power BI and I enjoy teaching tech to young learners, because I believe knowledge grows when it's passed on. Beyond the technical side, I'm interested in how technology fits into wider systems like policy, regulation and auditing, and how it can make institutions more transparent and forward-looking. I'm committed to continuous learning and to doing careful, honest work that people can rely on.

Currently

I'm in my third year of a BSc Honours in Data Science and Systems at the University of Zimbabwe. Alongside my studies, I'm writing a book on Power BI and am now working on the chapter about creating visuals. I'm also building and refining my Real Estate Management System with Flask and MySQL, and reviewing machine learning fundamentals through a structured, step-by-step approach. I'm preparing a policy research proposal on regulatory sandboxes for POTRAZ's Digital Technologies Policy Research Programme. I'm also open to internships, freelance projects and collaborations.
